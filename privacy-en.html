Aydan Aya · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · App: Aydan Aya: Baby Tracker (Android) · Türkçe · Русский

Aydan Aya is an app that helps you follow your pregnancy and your baby’s growth, vaccinations and development. This page explains which personal data we process, why and how, and what your rights are. It is based on Turkey’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK); where the data protection law of the country you live in (for example the GDPR in the EU and UK) gives you more protection, we respect those rights too.

In short: your data is only used to make the app work. We don’t show ads, we don’t sell your data, and we don’t share it with advertising or analytics companies. You can delete your account and your data from inside the app at any time.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Data controller: İnan Tümüç
Contact: aydanaya.destek@gmail.com

2. What data we process

DataExamples
Account detailsYour name and email address; with Google sign-in, the name and email on your Google account. We never receive your password in readable form.
Baby detailsName, date of birth and sex; length, weight and head measurements; vaccination and check-up records; developmental milestones and teeth; sleep, feeding and nappy log; memories and notes.
Pregnancy detailsDue date or date of last period, week of pregnancy, weight records, kick and contraction counts, check-up and screening records.

Health data: pregnancy, measurement, vaccination and check-up information is a special category of personal data. We process it only to the extent you enter it in the app, with your explicit consent, and only to provide the service to you.

3. Why we process it

  • To calculate growth charts, your week of pregnancy and the vaccination schedule,
  • To show vaccination, check-up and weekly reminders,
  • To back up your records and keep them in sync with your family members,
  • To run the Mums section, notify you when someone replies, and prevent misuse.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or marketing.

4. Where your data is stored and who it is shared with

  • On your phone: all your records are stored on your phone first. You can use the app without signing in.
  • Cloud (Supabase): when you sign in, your records and photos are stored on Supabase Inc. infrastructure over an encrypted connection, so they can be backed up and synced with your family. Photos are not public; only members of your family can see them.
  • Google: Google’s sign-in service is used for “Sign in with Google”, and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications.
  • Your family members: people you invite to your family can see and edit the baby and pregnancy records and photos.
  • Mums section: your questions and answers here are visible to other people using the app. Please don’t post anything that could identify you or your baby.

These service providers’ servers may be located outside your country. When you sign in and use cloud sync, you consent to your data being transferred abroad for this purpose. If you use the app without signing in, your data never leaves your phone. We don’t share or sell your data to anyone else. We may share it with competent public authorities only if the law requires us to.

5. How long we keep it

Your data is kept for as long as your account is open. When you delete your account, your account and personal details are deleted from the server. If there are no other members in your family, all of your family’s records and photos are deleted as well. If there are other members, the shared baby records stay with them.

6. Deleting your account and data

From inside the app

Profile → “Delete account and all data” → “Delete permanently”. Your account and all records and photos on your phone are deleted straight away; if no one else is in your family, the records and photos in the cloud are deleted too.

Without the app

Email aydanaya.destek@gmail.com from the address you use for your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We’ll delete your account and data within 30 days and let you know.

To delete just one baby’s records, use the “Delete [name]” option on that baby’s profile.

7. Your rights

You have the right to find out whether your data is processed, to request information, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, to know who it has been shared with, to object, and to seek compensation if you have suffered harm (KVKK Article 11, and the equivalent rights under the law of your country, such as the GDPR). You can take a copy of your data yourself via Profile → “Export backup”. For any other request, write to us at aydanaya.destek@gmail.com; we will reply within 30 days.

8. Children

Aydan Aya is made for parents and is not designed for use by anyone under 18 on their own. Information about a baby is entered only by their parent or carer.

9. Security

All communication between your phone and the server is encrypted (HTTPS). Data on the server can only be accessed by your account and your family members; this rule is enforced at database level.

10. Medical disclaimer

Aydan Aya does not give medical advice; its content is for general information only. Growth values are based on World Health Organization standards; the vaccination schedule follows the official programme of the country you choose in the app. Baby development videos and images are illustrative. Always talk to your doctor or midwife about any health decision.

11. Changes

When we update this page, we change the date at the top. We also announce important changes in the app.
